📌 How to get hired:

• Bid on Jobs: Browse "Available Jobs" → Place a bid → If seeker accepts, job appears in "My Jobs"

• Direct Hire: Customers can hire you directly from Marketplace → Requests appear in "Hire Requests" → Accept to start working

• Complete Jobs: After finishing work, click "Mark Complete" to finish the job

• Manage Services: Add, edit, or delete your services in "My Services" tab
